## Infographic 1: "The Phishing Attack Lifecycle"

**Title:** How a Phishing Attack Works — Step by Step

**Step 1 — RECONNAISSANCE**
Icon: 🔍
Text: Attacker researches target. Collects info from LinkedIn, company website, social media.

**Step 2 — CRAFTING THE BAIT**
Icon: ✉️
Text: Designs convincing fake email. Clones real brand visuals. Sets up fake domain.

**Step 3 — DELIVERY**
Icon: 📤
Text: Sends email to target. May use compromised email accounts for added legitimacy.

**Step 4 — THE HOOK**
Icon: 🪝
Text: Victim clicks link or opens attachment. Lands on fake page or malware executes.

**Step 5 — EXPLOITATION**
Icon: 💀
Text: Credentials stolen. Malware installed. Account taken over. Money transferred.

**Step 6 — MONETIZATION**
Icon: 💰
Text: Dark web sale of credentials. Ransomware payment demanded. Identity fraud begins.

## Infographic 4: "Red Flags Checklist" — Printable Card

**Format:** A5 card, dark background

```
PHISHING RED FLAGS CHECKLIST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EMAIL CHECK:
□ Sender domain matches the company exactly?
□ Addressed to you by your real name?
□ No urgency threats or artificial deadlines?
□ No requests for OTP, password, or payment?
□ Link URL checked by hovering first?

WEBSITE CHECK:
□ URL starts with https://?
□ Domain is exactly correct (no typos)?
□ No request for unusual information?
□ Site looks professional and complete?

PHONE/SMS CHECK:
□ Did you initiate this contact?
□ No request for OTP or passwords?
□ No request for gift cards or crypto?
□ Number matches official website?

GOLDEN RULE:
When in doubt → Go directly to the
official website. Never via a link.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

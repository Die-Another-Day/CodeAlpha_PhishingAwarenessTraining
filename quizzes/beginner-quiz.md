## Section B: Scenario-Based Questions

---

### Scenario 1: The Urgent IT Request

You receive this email at work:

```
FROM:    it-support@company-helpdesk.net
TO:      you@yourcompany.com
SUBJECT: Critical Security Update — Immediate Action Required

Hi,

Your workstation has been flagged for a critical security vulnerability. 
You must install the attached patch immediately. Failure to do so in 
the next 2 hours will result in your account being locked.

[Download Patch: security-update.exe]

IT Support Team
```

**Questions:**
1. List THREE red flags in this email.
2. What type of phishing is this, and what does the attachment likely contain?
3. What steps should you take?

---

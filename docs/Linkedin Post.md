## Option 2: Educational Awareness Post

---

🚨 **You've probably already received a phishing email today.**

Most people don't know how to spot them.

After completing my Cybersecurity Internship task on Phishing Awareness, here are **7 red flags that reveal a phishing email immediately:**

1️⃣ **Sender domain doesn't match** — `paypall-verify.com` ≠ PayPal
2️⃣ **Generic greeting** — "Dear Valued Customer" (no name = red flag)
3️⃣ **Artificial urgency** — "Act in 24 hours or account deleted!"
4️⃣ **Requests for OTP, passwords, or payment** — No legitimate service asks this via email
5️⃣ **Suspicious links** — Hover before you click. Always.
6️⃣ **Too good to be true** — Free MacBook for internship? Run.
7️⃣ **Grammar/spelling errors** — Professional companies proofread

✅ **When in doubt:** Close the email. Open a new browser tab. Go directly to the official website.
